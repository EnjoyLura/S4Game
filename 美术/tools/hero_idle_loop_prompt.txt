MANDATORY IDLE LOOP CLOSURE:
The video itself must be a complete natural forward-time cycle, suitable for
repeating in chronological frame order. Do not simulate a loop by playing motion
backward, retracing the same deformation, or pausing at a turning pose. Cloth-wave
propagation, inertia and overlap continue in natural forward time throughout.
Create one closed, repeatable idle cycle. Begin in the supplied relaxed ready pose,
complete one gentle breathing and cloth-motion cycle, and settle back into exactly
that same pose before the clip ends. The ending must match the beginning in head,
shoulders, hands, weapon direction, cape and robe silhouettes, feet, scale and framing.
Cloth must return with matching motion direction and gentle speed; do not leave the
cape or robe blown to a different side at the end. Camera, crop, floor position and
hand contact and held-prop positions remain fixed. Keep all non-cloth elements still,
with the same ordinary painted highlights and constant lighting. Only the existing
clothing surface moves; preserve every other element exactly as drawn.
Keep the complete character opaque on the unchanged solid background throughout.
Reserve generous empty background on both sides, below all cape tips and around the
entire moving silhouette. Every cloth tip must remain fully inside the frame for
the entire motion cycle. No body part, weapon, cape or robe may touch a frame edge.
End at the same cycle phase, with the same gentle motion direction and speed as
the opening. Keep moving through the final instant; do not add a static ending
hold, freeze, fade, dissolve or abrupt recovery. This is one complete idle cycle.
