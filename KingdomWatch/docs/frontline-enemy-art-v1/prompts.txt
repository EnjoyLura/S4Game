MELEE / 劫掠步卒
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original full-body enemy character sprite concept for the first chapter of a medieval frontier defense game, with genuine transparent background.
Input images: image 1 (orange-haired archer) is ONLY a reference for bold ink contours and broad painted color planes, NOT the character identity or pose; image 2 (outpost) is ONLY world palette/context.
Primary request: redesign the basic massed melee enemy as a HUMAN frontier raider footman, a low-rank expendable bandit, clearly readable at 80 pixels tall. One character only, a new original design.
Design: squat stocky three-head-tall adult human with a big wedge nose and simple scowling eyebrows; a battered low gray iron kettle cap hiding most hair; faded brick-red short tunic, oatmeal sleeves, one broad brown belt, dark charcoal trousers, chunky worn boots. Right hand holds ONE short heavy chopping blade low and away from the body. Left forearm carries ONE very small rough wooden buckler with a single broken upper edge: a scavenged prop, not an armored tank. Barely any armor. Asymmetric, practical, cheap soldier silhouette. No large cape. Enough negative gaps between arms, torso and legs for animation.
View and pose: elevated three-quarter FRONT view, camera looking down about 30 degrees, character faces toward lower right of the image (toward the bottom of a vertical game battlefield), head torso feet oriented consistently. Both eyes and face visible. A compact neutral advancing ready stance, both feet visible and planted, blade visible, full body centered, 12% transparent padding, square canvas. No action effects or floating debris.
Style: deliberately authored 2D hand-inked strategy-game cartoon, strong slightly uneven dark-brown outer contour, selectively thin interior lines, large deliberate shapes, two or three flat color values per material, tiny restrained brush accents. Simplify compared with the existing references. Matte painted surfaces. Graphic shape design comes first. Use a few asymmetrical nicks, not procedural texture. Warm muted red/brown/cream with cool gray steel. Head reads as an ordinary unscrupulous human, not a monster or hero.
Avoid: goblins, green skin, pointy ears, tusks, huge teeth, glowing eyes, fantasy horns, ornate engravings, many buckles, jewelry, skulls, photorealism, cinematic concept art, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, airbrushed gradients, rim light, noisy shading, thick fur, individual hair strands, generic hyper-detailed AI fantasy look, chibi baby face, extra weapons, text, labels, scenery, ground plate, cast shadow, watermark.
Output: one clean isolated whole character on real alpha transparency, usable as a model reference for later walk/attack animations.

RANGED / 投石散兵
Input image 1 is the companion melee footman: use ONLY as the exact art direction, palette, camera angle, outline treatment, scale and faction reference. Create a DIFFERENT character, a narrower leaner slinger; do not retain its helmet, blade or shield.
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original full-body ranged enemy character sprite concept on genuine alpha transparency, matching the frontier raider footman companion.
Primary request: a HUMAN frontier raider slinger, an ordinary low-rank ranged bandit. Preserve gameplay identity: fragile slow ranged soldier who stops behind the melee troops and throws stones with a sling.
Design: lean compact adult human, about three heads tall, narrower than the footman. Faded brick-red cloth hood covering hair, face fully visible, large simple wedge nose and wary scowling eyebrows. Oatmeal rolled sleeves, short dark-brown leather vest, red sash, charcoal trousers, plain worn boots. One chunky tan open stone pouch at hip with just THREE clearly drawn gray round stones. Right hand raised beside shoulder holding the two ends of a SHORT leather sling, its stone-loaded pocket hangs at elbow height, one clear U-shaped strap; left hand holds one plain gray stone low beside pouch. No blade, shield, bow, quiver or staff. Short hood with NO flowing cape. Simple lowly soldier, not an assassin/hero. Clear gaps around arms and legs.
View: elevated three-quarter FRONT view, camera looking down about 30 degrees, facing toward lower right image corner, consistent head/body/feet. Calm combat-ready planted stance, not actively shooting. Full body centered with 12% transparent padding on square canvas, feet all visible.
Style: match companion's bold dark-brown ink outlines and matte large flat color planes exactly. A deliberately authored 2D hand-inked strategy-game cartoon. Two or three values per material, little interior linework, simplified shape language that reads at 80 pixels tall, warm muted brick-red/cream/brown, gray stones. Human facial features, restrained expression. No all-over scratches or procedural surface detail.
Avoid: goblins, green skin, pointy ears, tusks, monstrous mouth, huge teeth, glowing eyes, horns, magic, skulls, trinkets, decorative engraving, many straps, dense detail, photorealism, cinematic concept art, 3D render, glossy plastic, airbrush gradients, rim light, baby chibi anatomy, long cape, weapons besides sling, extra limbs, text, labels, scenery, ground disk, shadow, watermark.
Output: one whole isolated ranged soldier with real transparent background, an animation model reference.
