Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high fidelity portrait mobile game screen, full-screen secondary page for selecting endless-challenge chapters on a world map.
Primary request: create ONE finished portrait UI mockup for the Chinese fantasy strategy game 王国守望, approximately 1:2 portrait. This replaces a three-tab chapter screen with a pannable world map that can eventually contain dozens or hundreds of chapters.
Image 1 is the original placeholder world-map artwork: use this connected map, its regions, coast, landmark design and comic painting as the underlying world. Show a medium zoomed view with varied terrain filling the viewport and continuing beyond screen edges, rather than fitting an entire hundred-chapter world into the viewport. Land is the main subject, not a front-facing tower.
Image 2 is the project's previous UI mockup: use ONLY its slate-blue header, warm cream information area, gold accents and green chamfered action button style. Do NOT copy its three chapter tabs, giant gate, currency counters, or long rules block.
Layout:
- Entire image is the game screen, no phone hardware, no surrounding mockup canvas or callout annotations.
- Compact fixed slate-blue top bar, about 8% of image height, with large gold back arrow at left, centered Chinese title "无尽挑战", and a compact gold-edged dark button at right reading "章节目录". Account for top safe-area padding. No currencies, no three-tabs row.
- Map occupies approximately 70% of the total height, from under the header to the lower fixed panel. Pan-ready full bleed world art, no outer parchment frame. Show connected plains, forest, cave highlands, marsh, canyon/desert and hints of snow/volcanic distance; the map extends past viewport edges. Use enough close-up scale that roads, fortifications and chapter pins remain readable on a phone.
- Overlay EIGHT spaced chapter markers on appropriate clearings/landmarks along connected winding paths. One marker per chapter, NOT per endless floor. The first marker is an unlocked blue flag labeled "1". The second is the selected blue flag labeled "2", surrounded by a restrained golden selection ring. Above that selected flag only, a short dark plaque reads "深林". The third through eighth are locked gray shield/flag markers, with clear chapter numerals "3", "4", "5", "6", "7", "8" and small padlock symbols. Locked regions still visible but gently muted. Markers must not merge or collide with UI; large readable numbers. No stars, no floor numbers on map, no dense labels. No campaign stage dots numbered 1-1 etc.
- Just inside the lower-right MAP area, a small vertical stack of three distinct dark blue utility buttons: plus, minus, and a target/crosshair for returning to selected chapter. Subtle and thumb-friendly; they do not obscure a chapter flag.
- Bottom approximately 22% is an anchored full-width cream information bar separated from map by a thin gold divider. This is part of page, NOT a popup, no huge enclosing frame, no floating modal, no close X. Keep it compact, with only: title "第2章 · 深林挑战", next line "最高已通 15层", third smaller line "怪物范围：第1～2章". Below these, a wide single green chamfered button reading "开始挑战". Button and content have comfortable spacing, contrast and large type. Bottom safe-area padding.
Typography: render all requested Simplified Chinese accurately, clean bold dark blue text on cream, warm white title on blue. No promotional taglines, no English, no additional tutorial sentences or descriptions, no tiny unreadable numbers.
Art direction: clear comic fantasy map and illustrated game UI matching references, sharp dark contours, restrained texture, warm stone, forest greens, turquoise rivers; original geography. Keep map selection visually dominant and reserve gold highlight for selected marker. No glossy neon, no purple jewel controls, no photorealism, no copying Kingdom Rush interface.
This is a concept with temporary names and records; draw only the specified interface, do not print a "mockup" watermark or implementation text.
