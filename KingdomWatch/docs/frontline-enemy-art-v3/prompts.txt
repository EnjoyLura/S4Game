MELEE / 荒沟鼠兽近战
Use case: stylized-concept.
Deliverable: ONE full-body enemy model image for a medieval frontier defense game, for later AI-video animation. This is a redesign after the previous version was rejected as too cute, round, clean and generically AI-painted.
Subject species: an original vicious non-human DITCH VERMIN, a scavenging ratlike fantasy beast. NOT a human or humanoid in an animal costume; rat skull, long crooked animal snout, exposed dark narrow animal nose, small uneven incisors, small ragged asymmetric ears, sharp clawed paws, coarse patchy charcoal-brown coat, thin bare rat tail visible to one side. No goblin/orc face.
CRITICAL SHAPE DESIGN: ugly, mean, wiry, hungry, shabby lowly pest. A long narrow wedge-shaped muzzle, very small deep-set eyes under a low sloping brow, tiny uneven ears, an undersized head only ONE FIFTH of total standing height (4.5–5 heads tall), narrow chest, hunched raised shoulder blades, long skinny forearms, prominent angular elbows and knees, long claw feet. Compact narrow silhouette with slight asymmetry in shoulders, head kept front-facing. No broad barrel chest, no stuffed toy proportions, NO giant head, NO round face, NO huge paws, NO tiny cute legs, NO plush fur, NO big expressive white eyes, NO adorable cheeks. Forearms and claws have animal anatomy and four digits, no human hands. No hero physique.
Visual design: deliberately drawn 2D ink-and-gouache game cartoon, sharp irregular brush contour, lively broken interior ink strokes, large mostly FLAT matte color shapes, one shadow value and one base value per material. Very sparse scruffy fur not individual hairs. Dry-brush marks only on a few frayed edges. One small readable ear notch and a crooked cloth hem provide history, not hundreds of random scratches. Muted charcoal, dirty taupe, dusty desaturated rust cloth, bone beige, old blackened brown wood. Rough, unglamorous, nasty, exhausted by life; not friendly, not elegant, not shiny. Graphic and readable at 80–100 pixels tall. As if designed by a human animation character artist who edits out unnecessary detail. NO faceted polygons filling every surface, NO all-over triangular highlight patches, NO glossy airbrush shading, NO photorealistic textures, NO 3D, NO dramatic lighting, NO cinematic detail, NO decorative armor or jewelry, no franchise costume or recognizable existing character.
Pose and camera: strictly DIRECT FRONT VIEW looking straight at the viewer, full body centered, eye-level orthographic camera; head, chest, pelvis and both toes face camera. No three-quarter view, no side view, no elevated view. Neutral ready pose, not attacking or walking. Both feet planted on the same baseline. Arms held away from body so hands and weapons are clear for animation; slim leg gap. Entire ears, weapon, claws, feet, tail visible, generous 12 percent empty canvas margin. Square composition, one character only.
Background: completely plain opaque flat solid bright GREEN chroma background, edge to edge, no scenery, no floor, no horizon, NO ground shadow, no backdrop gradient, no vignetting, no texture, no green spill on character, no transparent checkerboard. Character has no green color. No particles, no magic, no VFX. No text, title, label, watermark or multiple views. No gore or exposed wounds.
Role: ordinary disposable MELEE scavenger from a frontier raiding pack.
Equipment: one tattered dirty desaturated rust waistcloth with an uneven patched hem, one simple cord belt; naked animal chest and limbs, patchy scruffy fur. Image left paw holds ONE crude short crooked blackened wooden club pointing down and slightly outward, with a single jagged split and a simple wrap near the grip. Image right paw holds ONE small broken rectangular plank shield with a large missing corner, roughly bound by two dark rope bands, NO round metal boss, NO neat rivets, no polished metal. Tiny ugly rag scrap tied at one elbow. No full suit, no helmet, no boots. The shield must remain small so this still reads as weak swarm fodder, not a tank. Front teeth barely show in a tight mean snarl, lips animal not human. Keep face head deliberately SMALL, long muzzle has most of its width.
