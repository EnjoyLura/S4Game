Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE new FIRST CHAPTER ENEMY ROSTER concept sheet, six original enemies in a 3-column x 2-row grid, landscape canvas. These are NEW DESIGNS for an existing fantasy defense game, not goblin reskins.
Reference images: three supplied later-chapter monster sheets from the SAME game. Use them only for illustration language and quality: assured ink outlines, chunky anatomical masses, broad painted shadow facets, grounded materials, fierce expressive silhouettes. Do not copy their creatures, costumes or layouts' dark backgrounds.
Backdrop: ONE absolutely flat light warm ivory (#EEE8DA). No gradient, vignette, texture, ground, shadow, text, captions, panels, border, circles, UI or effects floating around characters.
Roster concept: “BROKEN-FENCE WILDLINGS”, hungry intelligent nonhuman beasts raiding a woodland frontier's farms, supply carts and wooden outposts. Their own anatomy performs each combat role; NOT generic muscular human bodies wearing animal heads. Shared habitat and materials: coarse brown/grey/ochre fur, rust-red mane, roots, grubby scavenged sackcloth, weathered fence timber, small rope lashings. Very limited scavenged props; most of the design is purposeful silhouette and anatomy. A coherent low-level early-game threat, not a set of decorated legendary heroes.
Composition: exactly SIX entire independent monsters. Upper-left, upper-middle, upper-right; lower-left, lower-middle, lower-right. Full bodies point and look STRAIGHT TOWARDS VIEWER, eye-level frontal game sprite view. All legs, paws, tail tips and equipment fully in their own cell. At least 8% clean margin from every outer image boundary and between units; especially the lowest paws and tallest ears/wood. Each unit fills about 76-82% of own cell. No overlapped units. A planted neutral alert pose suitable as later animation reference, no lunging, extreme foreshortening or profile. Do NOT draw generic goblin ears, green humanoids, humans, or any human armor torso.
EXACT SIX DESIGNS, distinguish each at tiny in-game size:
TOP LEFT, ordinary melee “断齿獾”: a LOW BROAD QUADRUPED digging badger-beast, triangular wedge torso, surprisingly small flat head nearly level with heavy shoulders, pale broken stripe across brow, broad short muzzle, a SINGLE chipped yellow lower tusk, small dark amber eyes. Thick short forelegs with THREE large shovel claws and small rear legs. Grey-brown shaggy coat with dark layered shoulder patches. One tiny torn ochre supply-sack neck wrap, nothing else. Grumpy determined creature, not a smiling cute baby, no huge glossy eyes. Its digging paws and low wedge identify cheap close-combat fodder. No held weapon.
TOP MIDDLE, ordinary ranged “囊颊掷石兽”: completely different SILHOUETTE, narrow upright haunches beneath huge round cheek-storage pouches and long dexterous forearms; a marmot/porcupine-like scavenger creature with small pointed muzzle, short rounded ears, coarse rusty brown fur and a short quill ridge down the back. Its body is an animal's long pear shape, no human chest. One oversized stone-filled scavenged cloth pouch lashed to its flank. One forward-facing paw visibly holds a blunt grey stone, other paw hangs open beside body; three fingers and thumb clearly grasp stone. Visible mouth cheeks asymmetrically bulge with spare pebbles. Long tail curves down one side within cell. Body proportions are wiry under large cheeks, not cute plush round. Plain functional stone thrower, not a mage or archer.
TOP RIGHT, healing support “芽背疗兽”: a small-to-medium low long-backed QUADRUPED woodland shrew/marten-like beast with a long tapered snout and narrow wise eyes, dark desaturated moss-brown coat, thin swept-back whiskers, bent sturdy paws. The animal is wary and slightly sour, NOT a cute pet; no baby head, smiling eyes, deer antlers or wizard clothes. It visually secretes healing sap; no staff, floating runes or sparkling particle cloud.
BOTTOM LEFT, fast melee “赤鬃裂牙豺”: lean muscular adult QUADRUPED hyena-like raiding beast, long dropped muzzle, narrow waist, disproportionately high shoulder ridge, lower hindquarters, elongated forelegs, small rear ears, four clearly separated paws. Face-on and planted, tail kept inside cell, NOT a wolf puppy or dog mascot. Its long legs and shoulder-to-hip slope communicate speed.
BOTTOM MIDDLE, heavy elite “破栅撞兽”: a VERY WIDE LOW QUADRUPED wild-boar monster with a blunt flat shovel-like snout, thick neck and extremely heavy forequarters, short bristly legs and cloven hooves, tiny eyes deep in brow, TWO stubby irregular outward tusks; brown-black body. BOTTOM RIGHT, chapter boss “断木熊魁”: a TOWERING HEAVY OLD BEAR-BEAST, rectangular front silhouette with enormously long heavy natural forearms, high hunched shoulder mound, thick short hind legs, a low-set SMALL bear head deep under shoulders, broad short bear muzzle, one old pale scratch over the brow, one folded ear, dark amber close-set eyes. Stands upright on planted natural bear hind paws, one giant clawed forepaw down and open; other forepaw grips ONE short uprooted broken frontier-gate post held LOW TO THE SIDE, entire post visible, a thick dull iron staple still in timber. A tiny torn red supply banner tied to post reinforces raiding theme, not a cape or royal costume. No crown, chest gem, skeletons, human plate armor or gold. Old weight, restraint and authority; not roaring horror, not a fluffy friendly bear.
Design/rendering priorities: adult stylized fantasy anatomy; distinct faces, mass distributions and silhouettes. TWO OR THREE large shadow steps, medium sharp ink contour, painted material changes INSIDE clear masses. Functional wear and asymmetric damage, no decorative clutter. Clear eyes with small pupils, closed or barely open mouths. Rugged lively antagonists suitable for a colorful frontier defense game: neither grotesque realistic horror nor chibi cuteness. NO gore, exposed flesh, wide nightmare mouths or overgrown jagged teeth. Each creature must look intentionally designed for a specific game role, not random fantasy adjectives, not six variants of the same monster. Match the later-chapter references' art quality while inventing this new first-chapter roster.
