Nose sits on the exact middle axis of the face, eyes on one horizontal line, both ears equally visible and spread to the sides, both shoulders equally visible, equal widths of left and right cheek. No triangulated shading, no polygonal patches, no mottled skin, no busy rock-like highlights, no gradients, no airbrush, no glossy 3D lighting, no beveled limbs, no photoreal details, no rim light. Simple DOES NOT mean a vector pictogram or a preschool sticker: give it a strong game-ready personality and solid drawing.
