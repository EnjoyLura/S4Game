BANDIT / 哥布林土匪
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE original enemy character model image for the existing medieval frontier tower-defense game.
INPUT IMAGE 1 is the existing game monster atlas, used ONLY as the art-direction reference: bold dark-brown ink outlines, chunky readable anatomy, matte hand-painted color shapes, a few sculpted highlights, used leather and dull metal, colorful fantasy adventure rather than horror or a preschool cartoon. Match its drawing finish and level of stylization so the new creature belongs in the same game. Do NOT make a collage, reuse any existing enemy, or reproduce a commercial franchise creature. Exactly ONE subject.
Visual hierarchy: strong silhouette and a handful of purposeful details. Neither plush/cute nor realistically terrifying. No round teddy face, giant sparkling eyes, baby proportions, big smile, glossy plastic, 3D render, cinematic lighting, excessive fur detail, random geometric facets, decorative particle effects, gore or wounds. Some wear is welcome, no all-over texture noise.
Framing: strictly frontal, looking directly at camera, head and torso face the viewer, no side or three-quarter view, no overhead camera. The full body and all equipment or wings fit inside the image with at least 12 percent clear margin. No labels, text, border, ground disk, ground shadow, props on the ground, vignette or background scene. Opaque perfectly plain single-color background, no gradient, texture or transparent checkerboard. This is a still neutral animation-model first frame, not a dynamic attack.
Subject: a new GOBLIN BANDIT, a non-human frontier highway robber from the existing green goblin population. Compact lean adult build about 3.5 heads tall, ordinary low-tier enemy, not a king or elite. Green face, angular long ears, pointed nose, tiny sly eyes and two small uneven teeth, surly guarded expression. A worn dark brown cloth hood and short ragged shoulder cape, patched russet sleeveless leather jerkin, dark cloth trousers, low mismatched battered boots, simple leather belt and ONE small stolen coin pouch. No ornate armor, jewels or emblems.
One hand holds ONE short broad dull iron knife DOWN by the side, clearly separated from the torso. The other hand is an empty slightly bent clawed hand. Feet planted on one level baseline, arms separated from torso, calm ready pose. One torn hood edge, one cloth patch, a nick on the blade, a little dust on knees and boots. The hood must NOT obscure the distinctive goblin face or both ears.
Background: pure uniform bright BLUE chroma key #0055FF, completely opaque and flat, no blue spill light on character. The green skin must remain green. Single full-body standing goblin bandit.
