Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE new enemy concept for a hand-painted 2D fantasy tower-defense game: a BONE-GNAWING WARG, a feral quadrupedal assault beast used by the same rough beastfolk tribe. Full-body creature on a flat solid warm ivory background.
Reference roles: Image 1 is the user's wolf-rider image: use ONLY THE WOLF for animal anatomy, heavy jaws, dark fur shapes and the practical worn leather/bone material language. Do not include the rider. Image 2 is a recent thrower drawing: use ONLY its dark contour weight, simplified painted color blocks and game-art finish, not its humanoid anatomy, blue skin or costume. This is a wolf monster, not a humanoid or a gorilla.

Design: a low forward head nested into powerful high shoulders; deep narrow chest, lean tucked flanks and springy bent hindlegs, long thick animal forelegs ending in large wolf paws. Dark slate-charcoal fur with a muted plum undertone, light ash-grey cheek ruff and lower muzzle. A broad blunt lupine muzzle, black centered nose, small narrow amber eyes under a hard brow and two prominent worn ivory upper canines visible in a restrained half-snarl. Mouth is only partly open: tough, irritable hunting beast, not a screaming demon and not a cute pet. Strong animal muscles suggested with a few broad shapes, not human pectorals or sculpted abs. Fur grouped into 5-7 readable coarse locks along the shoulders and cheeks, not hundreds of tiny strands. One nicked ear and one slightly chipped fang give natural individual character.

Equipment: a battered dark-brown rawhide collar and one loose diagonally crossing chest strap, a few dull iron fastenings. Two small scavenged bone toggles tied off-center at the collar and a short frayed ochre cord near one ear. Head and face remain mostly unobstructed. Practical restrained tribal handling gear, no skull helmet, no giant pauldron, no cape, no human clothing, no decorative blanket, no rider or saddle. Worn leather has a few cracks and one uneven damaged edge, not a clutter of patches. Let fur silhouette, broad jaws and paws carry the design.

CRITICAL CAMERA: exact FRONT VIEW. Head, muzzle, chest and longitudinal body axis point straight toward camera. Nose centered between equally visible eyes; both ears visible. Absolutely no side face, no three-quarter angle, no turned head. Neutral standing quadruped, front paws grounded at the same depth, hind legs naturally behind and visible to either side of the forelegs. Tail remains visible curling a little to one side behind the body. All four feet, ears and tail fully within frame. Head held low naturally without tilting upward. No leaping, running or dramatic perspective.
Style: cohesive with the references, confident varied dark outlines, broad muted painted blocks, just a few decisive shadows, rough utilitarian materials. Readable at small game-sprite size. Face is the focus, gear secondary. Avoid shiny 3D gradients, over-rendering, excessive spikes, glowing body veins, glossy fur, mirrored ornament patterns or horror gore.
Composition: one centered complete monster, filling roughly two-thirds of the square canvas with generous empty margin on ALL sides for future animation. Plain uniformly colored ivory background, no paper texture, no scenery, no banner, no labels, no text, no watermark.
